# Security policy

## Reporting a problem

If you find a security problem in Lights Out, please report it privately:

**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/Chester-xx/lights-out/security/advisories/new)**

Do not post security problems as public issues, and do not share them anywhere else before they are fixed.

Please include:

- what you found and where (the page or the address),
- the steps to repeat it,
- what could happen because of it.

## What is covered

The Lights Out website at lightsout.org.za and the service behind it.

## Please do not

- read, change or delete other people's data,
- send large amounts of traffic or try to take the service down,
- send automated spam through the report form,
- include real personal information of other people in your report.

## What to expect

Lights Out is a student project run in spare time. Responses depend on the maintainer's available time, so no response time is promised. There is no reward or payment for reports.

## Supported versions

Only the live website is supported.
