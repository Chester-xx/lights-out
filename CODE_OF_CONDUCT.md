# Code of conduct

This repository is for reporting problems and suggesting improvements to Lights Out. Everyone who takes part is expected to keep it respectful.

## What is expected

- Be polite and stay on the topic of the issue.
- Criticise ideas, not people.
- Respect other people's privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information.
- Write in plain language so more people can follow along.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, threats, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Posting other people's personal information.
- Spam, advertising or posts that have nothing to do with Lights Out.

## What happens when it is broken

Comments or issues that break these rules can be edited, hidden or deleted, and people who keep breaking them can be blocked. To report a comment or an issue, use the Report content option that GitHub shows on it. Decisions depend on the maintainer's available time.
